# Addition of two integers 
The addition of two integers is taking two numbers, negative or positive, and combining them to crate a sum.
For example:
1+1=2
Given the integers 1, and 1 again, when you add them you get 2.

As stated you can also add negative numbers. 
For example:
-3+-4=-7
Given the integers -3, and -4, when you add you get -7.
